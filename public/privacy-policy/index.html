3. Information we collect

3.1 When you contact us

Through our website form: the programme you're interested in, your name, email address and your message. By email or WhatsApp: your contact details and what you send us. Your name and email are needed so we can reply. The rest is optional.

Please don't include medical details in the contact form, email or WhatsApp. If you start coaching, we'll ask for what we need through our secure intake process.

3.2 When you join a coaching programme

  • Contact details, date of birth or age, and occupation.
  • Health and lifestyle information: health goals, relevant medical conditions, medication, allergies and intolerances, weight and body measurements, eating patterns, activity, sleep, stress and other wellbeing information you choose to share.
  • Session notes, food diaries, progress records and messages with your coach.
  • Billing information (invoices and payment confirmations). {{We do not store card details.}}

Health information is special personal information under POPIA. We only process it with your explicit consent (POPIA s27(1)(a)), which we ask for in writing before your first session. You can withdraw it at any time, although we may then be unable to continue coaching you safely.

3.3 Employee Wellness Programmes

If your employer has arranged a programme for you, we receive your name, work email and department from your employer to enrol you. Anything you share with us about your health stays confidential between you and NutriFYI. We only give your employer anonymised, combined (aggregate) information, such as attendance numbers or overall feedback, which cannot identify you, unless you give us separate written permission.

3.4 Membership (coming soon)

When the membership launches, we will collect account details (name, email, password), your learning progress, and subscription and payment records. We will update this policy before launch.

3.5 Information collected automatically

Our website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Our web host, Netlify, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) for security and to keep the site running.
