Our story

Why we created NutriFYI

You probably already know what you should be doing: eat better, move more, sleep well, stress less.

But knowing isn’t the hard part. Doing it is. Work runs late, your family needs you, stress builds, and the plan you started with such good intentions quietly slips away. Before you know it, you’re back at square one, frustrated and wondering why you can’t just stick to it.

If that feels familiar, you’re not alone. After years of coaching people through challenges with eating habits, stress, energy, movement and self-awareness, we’ve heard the same words again and again:

Here’s what we want you to know: it’s rarely about willpower. You don’t need to try harder. You need an approach that works with your life, not against it.

That’s where we come in.

Between us, we bring years of hands-on coaching, a degree in nutrition and postgraduate training in sports nutrition. What truly drives us, though, is the human side of health: the habits, thought patterns and everyday moments that shape how we care for ourselves. We’ve walked through our own challenging seasons too, so we understand that health is rarely a straight line.

With a practical, science-informed approach, we’ll meet you where you are and help you close the gap between knowing and doing, one manageable step at a time. Our goal is to help you feel more energised, more in control and more like yourself again, without starting over every time life gets in the way.

Build on what works. That’s NutriFYI.
Our approach

Small shifts that fit real life

You don’t have to overhaul your life to make a meaningful difference to your health. Routines shift, bodies change, and what feels possible in one season may look different in the next.

So instead of starting from scratch, we start with you.

1

Recognise what’s working

The habits and strengths you already have, and can build on.

2

Understand what gets in the way

The patterns, pressures and everyday moments that knock you off track.

3

Build from there

Practical, manageable habits that fit the life you’re living now.

Health isn’t a competition or a fixed destination. Your best will change, and that’s OK. It’s about what works for the person you are today.
